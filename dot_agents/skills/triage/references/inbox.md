# Inbox screening

List open card IDs, titles, statuses, labels, and reconsideration notes. Record the audit set
before editing. Include unreviewed Inbox cards and previously screened captures
whose evidence or proposed scope changed. A capture awaiting a batch decision with
no new evidence stays pending without being investigated again.

Inspect every `deferred` card's `Reconsideration` section, in any column. Include a
card when its check date is due, its recorded event is observed, or its next check date or
event check is missing. Read event checks at each intake pass so a fulfilled prerequisite
can return before its fallback date. A missing service or failed probe stays
unverified. Give an unmet wait its next check date and preserve the reason.

Read existing open, completed, and archived titles and search by outcome and symptom
before proposing new work. Read the matching records in full. A title match alone
proves neither duplication nor completion. On an unmigrated board, include drafts
captured under the board admission policy.

Apply the common steps to this audit set. When answering needs a separate
investigation, propose it instead of expanding screening into a build. Compare
proposed priorities with accepted work and name anything displaced.

Prepare one batch table with card, evidence verdict, disposition, proposed scope,
priority, displaced work, and unresolved decision. Mark which rows need admission
and which changes were applied within existing authority. Record the typed batch
decision on the affected cards before applying it. Re-read changed premises first
when the decision arrives later. A decision approves the displayed scope, not
requirements added afterward.

Check the next accepted action against current code, resources, and dependencies.
Keep the accepted order unless new evidence changes readiness or priority. If it
cannot proceed, record the deferral under the board policy and inspect the next accepted candidate until one is feasible or the
accepted queue is exhausted. Write that card's bet with the goal observation,
check, and authorized budget, and attach the intake doc for its next session.
Pending admissions do not prevent that handoff.

Recommend full-board planning when a changed goal, milestone, or reflection requires
replanning. Do not silently expand this intake pass into a full audit. With no
recorded goal, finish factual screening and leave goal-based admission and ordering
pending a decision.

The intake doc records its frozen audit set, applied changes, pending batch,
returning and future deferrals, new arrivals, and next accepted action. Carry the
goal forward so the next triage or reflection can recover it. Record the number of
pending captures and oldest capture date, and any repeated duplicate or rewriting
problem. Use these observations and the user's correction burden to judge whether
intake is helping. Fewer cards alone is not evidence of success.
