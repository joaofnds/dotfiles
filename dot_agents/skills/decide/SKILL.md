# Decide

A question handed back costs a round trip and lands on the person with the least
context for it. Most of what a session asks is already answered, in
`~/.agents/rulebook/engineering.md`, the rest of the rulebook, the
project's documents, and the wiki the rulebook's using-the-wiki file describes.
Read those first.

## Answer before you spawn

Write the question down. Add the goal as it reached this session, each constraint,
and what a wrong answer costs to reverse. Each constraint names its backing, which
is a direction, a measurement, or nothing. A question that turns on a fact a probe
can settle is not a question. Run the probe, and the result answers.

Then answer from the sources. Survey a choice between ways to build a thing the way
the judgment file's Find the box bullet and the shape skill's Pick the approach,
then harden it describe, removal first. Write the answer and the source it rests on
down, on the card or in the turn's text, before the spawn call, because a session
that reads an advisor with no position of its own adopts the advisor's, and the
check then confirms nothing.

## Brief the advisor blind

Spawn the `advisor` agent fresh. Give it the question, the goal in the words it
reached this session, the constraints with their backing, and what reversing a wrong
answer costs. Point it at the repository and the files. The card stays out, since
it carries your answer.

Leave out your answer, your reasons, the options you saw, and any adjective on one,
since each steers the advisor toward your answer or away from your blind spot. Put
this line in the brief: "This brief deliberately contains no answer and no lean.
Form your own from the sources and the code."

## Compare

A source the advisor names gets read, and a fact it reports gets re-run, before its
answer moves yours.

Where the advisor's answer matches yours, proceed. Where the reasons differ, record
both, since two reasons are two claims and not one confirmed. Where the answer
changes what the task delivers, the judgment file's Find the box bullet ends the
turn on it.

Where the answers differ, find the sentence where the two arguments part. Where they
part on a fact, probe it, and the probe decides. Where one answer is the removal
path, the Find the box bullet decides, and removal wins unless one of you can show
what rules it out. Where they part on a reading of a source, read the source again
with both readings in hand, and the source decides. A source that supports both
readings equally settles nothing, and the question goes back with the ambiguity
named beside it.

Where the advisor reports the question unsettled and your answer rests on a source
it did not name, continue it with that source, since a re-check is not a second
advisor. Where it stays unsettled, or the two of you part on a weight no source
sets, ask once. The question names what the sources lack, the two answers, and your
recommendation.

One advisor. A second one given both arguments is primed by both, and two advisors
of one model are one source counted twice.

The advisor never saw the argument for the answer, so the adversarial-review gate
still runs on a document that records it.

## Record

Record the advisor's answer and its source beside yours, marked whose is whose,
wherever the decision is recorded.
