---
name: brief
description: Re-renders the previous answer as a short brief a decision-maker can act on.
argument-hint: "What to shorten (default: the last answer)"
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Rewrite your most recent substantive answer, or the text João points at, in plain,
simple English, talking like an engineer talking to a CEO who has thirty seconds:
tell him only what he absolutely must know.

Open with one sentence saying what happened or what you found. Then one line each,
only where the answer has one: a decision he must make, with your recommendation; a
risk or open question; the exact thing he can do to unblock you. Cut method, tool
narration, and anything he cannot act on. Anything the answer called uncertain stays
uncertain in the brief. If nothing can be cut without losing something he must know,
say "nothing to cut" and stop.
